Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) visited the City of Subotica, where they held a meeting with senior representatives of ElevenEs, one of Europe's leading companies in the development and production of advanced battery technologies.

The discussions focused on opportunities for cooperation in the field of sustainable development, green technologies, and energy transition initiatives. Particular attention was given to the role of advanced battery systems in supporting the global shift toward cleaner energy solutions, as well as the potential for international collaboration in research, production, and industrial scaling.

During the meeting, EEAA representatives presented the organisation's activities and explored pathways for ElevenEs to engage more closely with the EEAA network, including potential membership and participation in joint international projects. The talks also highlighted opportunities for connecting European green technology companies with partners across Asia and other global markets.

The visit reaffirmed the shared commitment to innovation-driven growth and sustainable industrial development, with both sides expressing interest in further developing structured cooperation within future EEAA initiatives and cross-border green economy programmes.
