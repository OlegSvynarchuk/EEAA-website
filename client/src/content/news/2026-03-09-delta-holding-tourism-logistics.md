As European-Asian engagement increasingly emphasises practical partnerships, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with Delta Holding to explore areas of mutual interest, particularly in tourism, logistics and digital platforms.

The meeting highlighted Delta Holding's multifaceted operations, from hospitality and travel services to logistics infrastructure and e-commerce initiatives. Delegates discussed how these capabilities could be aligned with Asian market demands, fostering investment, joint projects and knowledge exchange. Particular attention was given to opportunities for integrating tourism and digital platforms, creating seamless experiences for international travellers and investors alike. Having in mind that Serbia and Bosnia and Herzegovina are amongst the countries that offer visa-free visits for Chinese tourists which are measured in millions and hungry for European experiences, it is only natural for this region to position itself as a tourist hub.

Also, being strategically located, South-East Europe is nothing less than a logical choice for a logistic connection between Asia and Europe.

Beyond business operations, the discussions explored the strategic role of Delta Holding as a regional connector, capable of linking local enterprises with international partners. The company's expertise in logistics and online platforms was presented as a key asset in enhancing connectivity, efficiency and market access across borders.

The exchange underlined the importance of moving from abstract dialogue to concrete initiatives, demonstrating that regional private-sector leaders, when engaged strategically, can play a central role in advancing European-Asian cooperation. By leveraging existing strengths and exploring innovative collaborations, Delta Holding and EEAA aim to translate shared ambitions into measurable economic and social outcomes.
