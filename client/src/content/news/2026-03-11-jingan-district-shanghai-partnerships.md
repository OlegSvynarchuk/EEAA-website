Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a high-level meeting with officials of the Jing'an District Government in Shanghai. The meeting was led by Mr. Mo, representing the Organisation Department of Jing'an District, and included participants from the District's Department of Culture and Tourism, Bureau of Sports, as well as the Jing'an District Development and Reform Commission.

The discussions focused on opportunities for institutional cooperation, investment promotion, and strengthening economic, cultural, and tourism ties between Jing'an District and EEAA member countries. Particular attention was paid to Jing'an's role as one of Shanghai's key urban and business centers, with strong potential for international partnerships in services, creative industries, sports, and sustainable urban development.

Following the official meeting, EEAA representatives took part in a guided tour of the core area of Jing'an District, where they were introduced to major development projects, commercial zones, and cultural landmarks. During the tour, a meeting was also held with representatives of the Shanghai Jing'an Commercial Association, providing an opportunity for direct B2B networking and discussions on concrete cooperation initiatives.
