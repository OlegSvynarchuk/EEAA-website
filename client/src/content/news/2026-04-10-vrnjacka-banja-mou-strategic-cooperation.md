The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) and the Municipality of Vrnjačka Banja have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) establishing a framework for strategic cooperation.

The agreement focuses on promoting Vrnjačka Banja as one of Serbia's leading spa, wellness, tourism and medical rehabilitation destinations on Asian markets. Under the MoU, EEAA will support the representation and promotion of Vrnjačka Banja across Asian markets, with a particular focus on medical cooperation, tourism, trade and other related areas.

The partnership is intended to strengthen international business and institutional connections, facilitate new opportunities for cooperation, and support the development of Vrnjačka Banja's presence among partners, investors, healthcare institutions and tourism stakeholders in Asia.

The agreement further expands EEAA's network of cooperation and contributes to stronger links between Serbian destinations and Asian markets.
