An online meeting was held between representatives of the Chamber of Economy of Montenegro and partners from China, focusing on strengthening bilateral cooperation in key economic sectors. The discussion highlighted opportunities for enhancing trade exchange, boosting tourism flows, and encouraging new investment initiatives.

Participants emphasized the importance of deepening economic ties through more dynamic business communication and the identification of concrete joint projects. Particular attention was given to the potential for increasing exports, as well as to promoting Montenegro as an attractive destination for Chinese tourists and investors.

The meeting also underscored the role of institutional cooperation in facilitating business connections and improving the overall investment climate. Both sides expressed readiness to continue dialogue and to organize follow-up activities aimed at translating mutual interest into tangible economic results.
