In a continuation of efforts to strengthen European–Asian cooperation, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with officials from the Faculty of Organisational Sciences (FON) to discuss collaboration in education and talent exchange with universities in Shanghai.

Having in mind intensive growth of Chinese universities and science and their ever expanding prestige, it is of crucial importance for Serbian and Central and Eastern Universities to maintain close relations with their Chinese counterparts. The talks focused on developing frameworks for student mobility, faculty exchange and joint research initiatives, highlighting the role of academic partnerships in building long-term networks between Europe and Asia. As Asian universities increasingly seek international collaborations, FON's expertise in management, technology and organisational studies positions it as a valuable partner in shaping future leaders.

Delegates emphasised the broader significance of educational cooperation, noting that knowledge exchange serves as both a catalyst for innovation and a bridge for cultural and economic understanding. By linking curricula, research agendas and practical internships across continents, such partnerships can provide students and professionals with unique global perspectives.

The meeting reinforced the view that European-Asian engagement extends beyond trade and investment, encompassing human capital development as a strategic dimension of sustainable cooperation. Concrete next steps included designing exchange programmes, identifying research priorities and establishing ongoing institutional communication channels.
