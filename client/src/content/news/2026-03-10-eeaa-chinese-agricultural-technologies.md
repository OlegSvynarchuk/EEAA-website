An online conference was held between the EEAA, in cooperation with the Ministry of Agriculture, and representatives of relevant Chinese institutions, focusing on current opportunities for cooperation in the agricultural sector.

The meeting addressed the application of modern technologies in agriculture, with particular emphasis on improving efficiency and mitigating the impact of labour shortages. Participants also discussed possibilities for joint participation in international agricultural fairs, as well as existing and potential trade opportunities between the two sides.

Special attention was given to regulatory and technical issues related to the certification of agricultural machinery and equipment, with the aim of facilitating market access and improving mutual understanding of standards and procedures.

The conference confirmed a shared interest in strengthening bilateral cooperation and continuing dialogue through concrete initiatives and future activities in the field of agriculture.
