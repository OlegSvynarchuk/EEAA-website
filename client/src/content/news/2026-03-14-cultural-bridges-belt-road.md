Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Mr. Yan, President of the International Metropolitan Division of the Shanghai International Cultural Exchange Association. The meeting was followed by an official lunch hosted by President Yan in the Lujiazui area, one of Shanghai's key financial and cultural hubs.

During the talks, the two sides discussed opportunities for strengthening international cultural cooperation, people-to-people exchanges, and joint cultural and creative projects between Shanghai and European partners. Particular emphasis was placed on cultural diplomacy, institutional partnerships, and the role of cultural exchange in supporting broader economic and business relations.

The Shanghai International Cultural Exchange Association, established in 1992, plays a significant role in promoting international cultural and artistic cooperation through exhibitions, performances, academic forums, and creative programmes both in China and abroad. The discussion also touched upon the Association's potential contribution to the Belt and Road Initiative, especially in connecting cultural institutions, creative industries, and metropolitan networks across Europe and Asia.

EEAA representatives expressed interest in developing long-term cooperation frameworks, including exchanges between cultural organisations, joint events, and platforms that link culture, creativity, and international business cooperation.
