The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) and the Novi Sad Fair have signed a cooperation agreement aimed at strengthening the international promotion of one of Southeast Europe's most important exhibition and business event organisers. The agreement establishes a framework for EEAA to support the representation and promotion of the Novi Sad Fair across selected Asian markets, fostering new opportunities for business networking, trade development, and international partnerships.

Through this cooperation, both parties will work to increase the visibility of the Novi Sad Fair among companies, institutions, and investors from Asia, encouraging greater participation in trade fairs, conferences, and specialised exhibitions held in Serbia. The initiative is expected to contribute to the internationalisation of business events and facilitate stronger economic links between the Western Balkans and Asian economies.

The partnership reflects a shared commitment to expanding business cooperation, supporting export-oriented industries, and creating new platforms for dialogue between companies from different regions. By connecting the Novi Sad Fair with EEAA's international network, the agreement represents an important step toward enhancing cross-border trade, investment promotion, and long-term economic cooperation.
