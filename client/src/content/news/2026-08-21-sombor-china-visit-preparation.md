The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with representatives of the City of Sombor to discuss preparations for an upcoming official visit to China.

The meeting focused on the organisation and objectives of the visit, with particular attention given to presenting Sombor as an attractive destination for investment and strengthening its visibility among potential Chinese partners and investors.

The discussions also covered opportunities to promote the city's economic potential, investment opportunities and development priorities within the framework of EEAA's network and cooperation with Asian markets.

The planned visit is expected to provide an opportunity for Sombor to establish new business contacts, present its investment potential and explore possibilities for future economic cooperation with Chinese institutions and companies.
