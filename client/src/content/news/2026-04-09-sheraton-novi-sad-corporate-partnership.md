Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with the management of Sheraton Novi Sad, during which the two sides agreed to establish a corporate partnership and cooperation.

Under the partnership, all EEAA premium members will be entitled to special accommodation rates at the hotel throughout the year, including during peak periods and so-called blackout periods associated with international fairs and major business events.

The partnership marks another important step in EEAA's efforts to connect the international business community with reliable local partners in the cities where the Alliance operates. Following the cooperation established with the Novi Sad Fair earlier this year, the partnership with Sheraton Novi Sad further completes the package of services available to EEAA members and guests.

Through this network, EEAA aims to provide its members with easier access to quality accommodation, business infrastructure and professional services, while offering preferential conditions tailored to the needs of the international business community.
