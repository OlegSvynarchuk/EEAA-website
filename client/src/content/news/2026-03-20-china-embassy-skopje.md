A meeting was held between representatives of the EEAA and the Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Skopje, with the participation of Economic Attaché He Binghua, focusing on the current state of economic cooperation.

The discussion addressed the identification of key challenges in cooperation between North Macedonia and China, including issues related to the visa regime and existing trade arrangements. Participants also exchanged views on opportunities for improving bilateral economic relations through enhanced trade cooperation and the further development of tourism and the wine sector.

In addition, the meeting covered the potential for participation in international trade fairs as a platform for promoting business cooperation and strengthening institutional and commercial ties.

The meeting confirmed mutual interest in continued dialogue aimed at improving cooperation frameworks and addressing practical obstacles to economic exchange.
