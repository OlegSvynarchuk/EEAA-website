A meeting was held between representatives of the EEAA and Ivan Bošnjak, State Secretary at the Ministry of Human and Minority Rights and Social Dialogue, focusing on areas of potential cooperation and joint initiatives.

The discussion addressed opportunities for cooperation on projects funded by the European Union, as well as initiatives aimed at networking cities across Europe and strengthening communication and cooperation among peoples in the region. Participants exchanged views on the importance of dialogue, inclusion, and institutional cooperation in supporting sustainable regional development.

Special attention was given to topics related to women's entrepreneurship and the position of women in the economy, including measures to support greater participation of women in business and decision-making processes.

The meeting confirmed mutual interest in continued cooperation and the development of concrete activities aligned with European values and policy frameworks.
