Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) visited the Science and Technology Park Niš, one of Serbia's leading innovation and technology hubs, where a series of meetings were held with business representatives and members of the Serbian-Italian Chamber of Commerce.

The discussions focused on opportunities for strengthening international business cooperation, supporting innovation-driven companies, and creating new connections between entrepreneurs, investors, and institutions from Europe and Asia. Particular attention was given to the role of technology, research, and entrepreneurship in fostering sustainable economic growth and enhancing the competitiveness of regional businesses in global markets.

During the meetings, participants exchanged views on potential joint initiatives in the fields of innovation, technology transfer, startup development, and international market expansion. The visit also provided an opportunity to present EEAA's activities and explore areas of mutual interest with companies operating within the Science and Technology Park ecosystem.

As a result of the discussions, EEAA received an invitation to establish closer cooperation with Science and Technology Park Niš, laying the foundation for future joint projects, business networking activities, and initiatives aimed at connecting innovative enterprises with partners across Europe and Asia.
