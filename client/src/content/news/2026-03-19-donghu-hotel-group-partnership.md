**Executive Summit:** Representatives recently held a high-level strategic meeting with the Shanghai Donghu Hotel Group to discuss the expansion of their luxury hospitality footprint and the integration of cultural heritage with modern tourism.

**Expansion Milestones:** The discussions highlighted the group's leadership in the regional market, particularly as Shanghai is projected to lead global hotel room additions in 2026 with over 7,400 new openings.

**Innovation Initiatives:** Both parties reviewed recent advancements in digital transformation and service upgrading, following the group's commitment to "upgrading to the core" as a driver of market value.
