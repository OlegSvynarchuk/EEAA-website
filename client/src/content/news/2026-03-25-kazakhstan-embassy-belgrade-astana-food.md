Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with the Embassy of Kazakhstan in Belgrade to further develop economic cooperation and create new opportunities for member companies. The meeting resulted in an agreement to organize the participation of EEAA members at an upcoming food and beverage fair in Astana.

This initiative is aimed at enabling companies to present their products directly to the Kazakhstani market, establish business contacts, and explore export opportunities in a rapidly growing economy. The Embassy expressed its support in facilitating connections with local partners and institutions, as well as providing guidance on market entry requirements.

EEAA representatives highlighted the importance of international trade fairs as a practical platform for market positioning, especially in sectors such as food and beverages, where direct presentation and networking are crucial. The Alliance will support participating companies through coordination, preparation, and on-the-ground organization.

The initiative reflects a shared commitment to strengthening economic ties and creating concrete business results, while positioning EEAA members more strongly in Central Asian markets.
