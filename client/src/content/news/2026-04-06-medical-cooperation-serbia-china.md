The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) organised an online meeting bringing together the Institute of Rheumatology in Belgrade and medical partners from China, with a focus on strengthening bilateral cooperation in the field of healthcare and medical innovation.

The discussion centred on potential collaboration in areas such as regenerative medicine, medical devices, rehabilitation technologies, and advanced therapeutic approaches. Participants exchanged experiences and explored opportunities for joint research, knowledge transfer, and institutional cooperation aimed at improving patient care and expanding access to innovative medical solutions.

Special emphasis was placed on the growing importance of international partnerships in modern healthcare systems, particularly in the development of rehabilitation programmes and the application of cutting-edge technologies in treatment and recovery processes.

The meeting marked an important step toward building structured cooperation between Serbian and Chinese medical institutions, with EEAA acting as a facilitator for future exchanges, pilot projects, and long-term institutional partnerships in the healthcare sector.
