Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Motion Magic Digital Entertainment Inc., a Shanghai-based digital entertainment and content company operating under the umbrella of Shanghai Media Group (SMG).

The discussions focused on cooperation opportunities in digital entertainment, immersive exhibitions, and advanced visual technologies, including VR/AR solutions, real-time rendering, and digital twin applications. Special attention was given to the use of digital content in cultural institutions, exhibitions, branding, and large-scale public and commercial projects.

Motion Magic Digital presented its technological capabilities in CG production and immersive digital environments, as well as its experience in delivering complex digital solutions for clients across multiple industries. EEAA representatives expressed interest in exploring joint projects that connect European cultural, creative, and commercial sectors with cutting-edge digital content and exhibition technologies.

The meeting highlighted shared interest in developing international cooperation in digital content creation and innovative exhibition formats, supporting the digital transformation of cultural and entertainment industries across Europe and Asia.
