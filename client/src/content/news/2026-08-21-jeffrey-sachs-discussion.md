The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a discussion with Professor Jeffrey D. Sachs, renowned American economist, academic and public policy expert and one of the leading figures in international analytics of business affairs.

The meeting provided an opportunity to exchange views on current global economic developments, international cooperation and the evolving relationship between Europe, the United States and Asia.

Particular attention was given to the changing global economic environment and the importance of strengthening dialogue and cooperation across regions. Discussions with internationally recognised experts such as Professor Sachs contribute to EEAA's broader efforts to facilitate the exchange of knowledge, perspectives and ideas among business, academic and institutional stakeholders.

The meeting represents another step in EEAA's ongoing engagement with leading international experts and its efforts to support constructive dialogue on the major economic and geopolitical challenges shaping global markets.
