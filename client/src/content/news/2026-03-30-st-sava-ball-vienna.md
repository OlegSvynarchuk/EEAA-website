Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) attended the annual St. Sava Ball in Vienna, celebrating Serbian culture and strengthening community ties. The event provided an opportunity for networking with prominent members of the Serbian diaspora, business leaders, and cultural figures.

EEAA emphasized the importance of fostering connections beyond the business sphere, highlighting cultural diplomacy as a valuable tool in supporting international cooperation and building relationships across Europe. Attendance at the St. Sava Ball reflects the Alliance's commitment to engaging with communities and promoting Serbian cultural and economic presence abroad.
