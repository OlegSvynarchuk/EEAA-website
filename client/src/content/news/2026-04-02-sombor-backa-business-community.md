Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) paid an official visit to the City of Sombor, one of the key economic and cultural centres of northwestern Vojvodina and Serbia. During the visit, EEAA representatives held meetings with the Regional Chamber of Commerce of Bačka, where discussions focused on opportunities for strengthening economic cooperation, supporting international trade, and promoting investment links between local businesses and partners from Asia and Europe.

The delegation also visited one of the region's leading companies, Sinagoga, gaining insight into its business activities, development plans, and potential for international partnerships. Particular attention was given to exploring opportunities for expanding cooperation in manufacturing, trade, and export-orientated sectors.

As part of the program, EEAA representatives visited the Dragić Winery, where discussions highlighted the growing potential of the Serbian wine industry, rural tourism, and agribusiness development. The visit provided an opportunity to showcase regional products and identify new avenues for cooperation in the fields of food production, tourism, and cultural exchange. Together, the meetings demonstrated the strong potential of the Bačka region as a dynamic partner for international business collaboration.
