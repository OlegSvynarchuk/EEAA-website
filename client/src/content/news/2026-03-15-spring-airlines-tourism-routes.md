Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Spring Tour Group, part of the Spring Airlines Group, one of the leading aviation and tourism companies in China and the country's largest low-cost airline operator.

The discussions focused on cooperation opportunities in the fields of air connectivity, tourism development, and the integration of aviation and travel services between China and European destinations. Particular attention was given to the potential for opening or expanding routes, joint promotion of tourism products, and collaboration with European tourism boards, tour operators, and destination management organisations.

Spring Airlines Group presented its low-cost operational model, strong digital platforms, and experience in serving large passenger volumes with high efficiency. EEAA representatives highlighted the growing interest of European destinations in attracting Asian travelers and emphasised the importance of affordable air travel, digital booking solutions, and coordinated destination marketing.

The meeting confirmed mutual interest in developing partnerships that could enhance passenger flows, support tourism recovery and growth, and strengthen long-term cooperation between Chinese and European tourism and aviation sectors.
