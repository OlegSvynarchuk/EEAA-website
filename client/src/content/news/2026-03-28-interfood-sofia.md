The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) took part in Interfood Sofia, showcasing the activities and initiatives of its member companies in the food and beverage sector. The event provided a platform for networking, knowledge exchange, and exploring new business opportunities within the Bulgarian and broader regional markets.

EEAA representatives highlighted the importance of international trade fairs in connecting companies with potential partners, distributors, and clients. Participation in Interfood Sofia reflects the Alliance's commitment to supporting member companies in expanding their presence across Europe and fostering stronger regional business cooperation.
