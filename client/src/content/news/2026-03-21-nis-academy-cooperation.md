A meeting between representatives of EEAA was held with professors from the Academy of Applied Technical and Preschool Studies in Niš, focusing on the development of institutional cooperation and future joint activities.

The discussion addressed the preparation of a memorandum of cooperation covering projects in the fields of ecology and information technologies. Participants also explored the potential organization of professional and academic conferences, as well as opportunities to strengthen cooperation between education, science, and the business sector.

Special emphasis was placed on the development of student internship programmes, with the aim of improving practical skills and employability. The meeting also highlighted the importance of expanding international cooperation through joint projects and partnerships.

Both sides expressed readiness to continue discussions and define concrete forms of collaboration aligned with shared strategic objectives.
