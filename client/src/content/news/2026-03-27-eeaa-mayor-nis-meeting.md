Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Dragoslav Pavlović, the Mayor of Niš to discuss potential avenues for local and international economic collaboration. The meeting focused on exploring opportunities to strengthen business ties, support regional development, and facilitate cooperation between EEAA member companies and local institutions.

The discussion underscored EEAA's commitment to promoting sustainable economic partnerships and expanding its network across key European cities, with Niš identified as a potential hub for future initiatives and collaborative projects.
