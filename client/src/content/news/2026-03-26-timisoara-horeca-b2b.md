The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA), in collaboration with the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Vojvodina, organized a series of B2B meetings in Timișoara focused on the HORECA sector. The initiative aimed to strengthen regional business ties and facilitate cross-border cooperation between Serbian companies and Romanian distributors.

During the meetings, participants explored opportunities for joint ventures, product distribution, and supply chain partnerships, with particular attention to innovative solutions in the food and hospitality industries. EEAA representatives highlighted the importance of these structured encounters as a platform for companies to showcase their products, establish direct contacts, and gain practical insights into the Romanian market.

The initiative also included discussions on strategies for coordinated market entry, emphasizing collaboration, knowledge exchange, and preparation for regional expansion. Both sides expressed commitment to maintaining ongoing dialogue and building sustainable business relationships, laying the groundwork for increased trade and investment in the HORECA sector.

This effort underscores EEAA's continued mission to support member companies in entering new markets and strengthening European business networks across Central and Eastern Europe.
