B2B meetings were held on 20–21 November 2025 within the framework of the Chamber of Commerce of Vojvodina, as part of the 8th Regional Business Forum, bringing together representatives of companies and institutions from Vojvodina and the wider region.

The meetings focused on economic potentials of Vojvodina and opportunities for strengthening regional cooperation. Discussions addressed key sectors of mutual interest, including logistics, export and import activities, and tourism development, with the aim of improving connectivity and business exchange across regional markets.

Participants exchanged information on current market trends, operational challenges, and possibilities for establishing new partnerships. Particular attention was given to the role of logistics infrastructure and trade facilitation in supporting competitiveness and cross-border cooperation.

The B2B format enabled direct communication between business representatives and contributed to the identification of concrete opportunities for future cooperation, reaffirming the importance of regional dialogue and institutional support in fostering economic development.
