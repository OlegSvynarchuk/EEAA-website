As European–Asian economic relations increasingly rely on practical cooperation, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with the Serbian Chamber of Commerce to exchange views on current trends and experiences in international economic engagement.

The discussion focused on the role of institutional platforms in facilitating cooperation, sharing best practices and improving connectivity between European and Asian markets. Particular emphasis was placed on the importance of experience exchange in navigating regulatory frameworks, market access and long-term partnership building.

The meeting underscored the value of structured dialogue between international organisations and national economic institutions as a foundation for sustainable European–Asian cooperation.
