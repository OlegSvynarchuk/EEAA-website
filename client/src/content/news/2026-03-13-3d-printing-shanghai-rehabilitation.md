Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Dr. Jinwu Wang, Chairman of the Shanghai Rehabilitation Medical Engineering Research Association, at the Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital, including a visit to its advanced 3D Printing Centre.

The discussions focused on innovation in rehabilitation medicine, digital healthcare, and the application of 3D printing technologies in orthopedics, assistive devices, and personalised medical solutions. Special emphasis was placed on translational medicine, smart rehabilitation equipment, and opportunities for international cooperation in medical engineering, research, and technology transfer between Europe and China.

Dr. Wang presented key developments in digital medicine and biomedical manufacturing, highlighting the integration of clinical practice, research, and industrial application within Shanghai's healthcare ecosystem. EEAA representatives expressed strong interest in potential collaboration in the fields of medical technology, rehabilitation equipment, elderly care solutions, and joint research initiatives, particularly those connecting academic institutions, hospitals, and industry partners.

The meeting confirmed shared interest in strengthening cross-border cooperation in advanced medical technologies and innovation-driven healthcare systems.
