Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a working meeting with Mr. Kokanović focused on strengthening cooperation between EEAA member countries across Eurasia and the Serbian diaspora. The discussions addressed key priorities, needs, and potential mechanisms for more structured engagement with diaspora communities as an important bridge for economic, cultural, and institutional cooperation.

Particular attention was given to identifying concrete business initiatives and development projects that could be implemented in partnership with diaspora networks, in coordination with relevant institutions, including the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The meeting explored how diaspora connections can be further leveraged to support trade, investment flows, and knowledge exchange between Serbia and partner countries in Eurasia.

Participants agreed on the importance of mapping priority sectors and regions where diaspora expertise and networks can contribute most effectively to international cooperation efforts. The meeting concluded with a shared interest in developing a more systematic framework for diaspora engagement within EEAA activities, aimed at translating connections into sustainable economic and institutional partnerships.
