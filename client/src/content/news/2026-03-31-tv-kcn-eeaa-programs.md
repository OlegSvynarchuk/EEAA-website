Teodor Kern, President of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA), and Jovan Cvetković, Vice President, appeared on TV KCN to present the Association's programs, membership benefits, and strategic objectives. While EEAA's current focus is on Central and Southeastern Europe, both leaders emphasized that the Alliance is open to cooperation with other parts of Europe and provides guidance on exporting and importing goods to China and the broader Asian market.

Kern and Cvetković highlighted a significant knowledge gap regarding trade procedures, logistics, and technological aspects of doing business in Asia. EEAA aims to address these challenges by supporting its members' participation in trade fairs across Asia, offering professional translation services, and providing insights into local business culture and Asian mentalities.

Kern stressed that provincial differences in China are so substantial that conducting business with northern provinces differs as much from southern provinces as doing business between Sweden and Greece. Understanding these regional and cultural nuances is essential for successful market entry. The Alliance also facilitates connections with Chinese legal firms, assisting members in company registration, compliance, and local representation.

Quality control and logistics support are additional services provided by EEAA, allowing members to conduct on-site inspections within 24 hours through established local networks. These resources are particularly valuable for companies initiating their first projects in China, where motivated Chinese partners and favourable tourist and business conditions - such as visa-free entry to Serbia, Bosnia, and partially Montenegro - offer unique opportunities for engagement in business and tourism.

By combining market knowledge, legal support, logistical coordination, and cultural guidance, EEAA ensures that its members can navigate the complexities of Asian markets effectively. Both Kern and Cvetković emphasized that Serbia's popularity in China, coupled with the Alliance's expertise, creates a strong foundation for sustainable business growth, export development, and mutually beneficial partnerships throughout Asia.
