As European–Asian partnerships increasingly focus on sustainable development, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with ESO Tron, a leading Central European company in recycling and renewable energy, to explore opportunities for collaboration and knowledge exchange.

The discussions highlighted ESO Tron's role as a regional pioneer, combining advanced recycling technologies with renewable energy initiatives. Delegates examined how the company's expertise could support joint projects, attract investment and promote sustainable practices across both European and Asian markets. Having in mind that some of the Far Eastern countries, including China, have made a spectacular progress in recycling and green technologies, this is of utmost importance for countries with relatively poor recycling record, such as Western Balkan countries.

Beyond technology, the meeting emphasised the potential for broader institutional cooperation, including capacity-building, joint research and the sharing of best practices. ESO Tron's leadership in Central Europe was presented as a model for how private-sector innovation can align with regional and international strategies for sustainability. The exchange underlined the importance of practical, project-orientated engagement between European and Asian actors, demonstrating that environmental innovation can be both economically viable and strategically relevant in fostering transcontinental cooperation.
