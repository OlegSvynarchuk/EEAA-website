Continuing discussions on European–Asian economic cooperation, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with the Development Agency of Serbia to exchange views on Serbia's investment landscape and international positioning.

The meeting provided an overview of key investment potentials across Serbia, with a focus on sectors relevant to Asian markets and long-term strategic partnerships. Discussions highlighted the importance of institutional coordination, transparency and targeted promotion in attracting sustainable investment.

The exchange reinforced the role of development agencies as central actors in translating international interest into concrete economic engagement.
