Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with the Embassy of Kazakhstan in Skopje, aimed at strengthening economic ties and facilitating the entry of member companies into the Kazakhstani market. The discussion focused on identifying concrete mechanisms for supporting businesses in their international expansion and improving connectivity between European and Central Asian markets.

Particular emphasis was placed on Kazakhstan as a rapidly developing economy with significant potential across sectors such as energy, infrastructure, agriculture, and trade. The Embassy expressed its readiness to support initiatives that would enable companies from the region to better understand local regulations, business practices, and investment conditions.

EEAA representatives highlighted the importance of coordinated market entry, noting that joint action and knowledge-sharing significantly increase the chances of success in complex and competitive environments. In that context, the Alliance was presented as a platform that prepares companies for expansion through education, networking, and institutional cooperation.

The meeting also explored possibilities for organizing business delegations, B2B meetings, and sector-specific initiatives that would further strengthen cooperation. Both sides agreed that there is strong mutual interest in deepening economic relations and creating sustainable partnerships.

This initiative represents another step in the Alliance's broader strategy to position its members in high-growth markets, with Kazakhstan recognized as a key gateway to Central Asia.
