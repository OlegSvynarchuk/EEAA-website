Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with Shanghai Fengyuzhu Culture Technology Co., Ltd., a leading Chinese company specialising in digital cultural exhibition and immersive experience systems.

The discussions focused on cooperation in the fields of digital culture, immersive technologies, and innovative exhibition formats for cities, museums, and brands. Special emphasis was placed on the use of VR/AR, 3D content generation, and spatial computing in urban storytelling, cultural heritage presentation, and destination branding.

Fengyuzhu presented its experience in developing urban digital experience spaces and large-scale immersive cultural projects, highlighting the potential for applying similar solutions in European cities, cultural institutions, and creative industries. EEAA representatives expressed interest in exploring joint projects that combine culture, technology, and tourism, particularly in the context of digital transformation and contemporary visitor experiences.

The meeting confirmed shared interest in cross-border cooperation at the intersection of culture, technology, and creative industries.
