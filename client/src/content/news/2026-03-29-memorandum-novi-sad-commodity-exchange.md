The European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) has signed a memorandum of understanding with the Novi Sad Commodity Exchange, marking a significant step in fostering collaboration between its member companies and regional market institutions.

The agreement aims to strengthen trade relations, facilitate market access, and create opportunities for joint initiatives in commodities and related sectors. EEAA representatives emphasized that the memorandum will serve as a platform for knowledge exchange, capacity building, and coordinated business development.

This partnership reflects EEAA's ongoing commitment to supporting its members in expanding their presence in regional markets, enhancing trade cooperation, and promoting sustainable economic growth.
