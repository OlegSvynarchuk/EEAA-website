Florian Fimbel, Vice-President of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA), shares his perspective on the Alliance's work since November 2025.

Since assuming the role of Vice-President of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) in November 2025, I have had the privilege of representing this dynamic institution from France. In an increasingly interconnected global economy, our collective mandate goes far beyond conventional trade facilitation — we are building enduring bridges rooted in mutual respect, shared prosperity, and long-term economic resilience.

**Expanding Horizons: Connecting Europe, Asia and Africa**

While EEAA's core mission centres on fostering economic, cultural, and scientific cooperation between European and Asian nations, my strategic vision from France focuses on expanding these vital intercontinental dynamics to encompass concrete initiatives across Africa. By leveraging our pan-European network and Asian partnerships, we actively deploy projects designed to address specific local territory needs.

These initiatives are crafted to directly enhance the daily lives and economic well-being of the communities where they are implemented. Whether focusing on local infrastructure support, sustainable growth, or capacity-building programmes, our efforts ensure that international trade translates into tangible, community-level progress.

**Core Operational Priorities and Strategic Initiatives**

- Market entry and strategic matchmaking: facilitating seamless business installations when companies expand into new international markets, offering tailored guidance to streamline setup and operational integration.
- Commercial partnerships for French enterprises: connecting French companies with vetted, trustworthy international commercial partners to secure growth and expand export horizons.
- Bespoke tourism and cultural exchange: developing curated business and cultural travel itineraries originating from France to foster direct interpersonal and institutional engagement.

**A Commitment to Trust, Transparency and Excellence**

"EEAA stands out because of the exceptional dedication of its members. Every partner within our alliance shares a sincere commitment to developing healthy, trust-based relationships that deliver tangible, equitable benefits to all parties involved."

What makes EEAA truly exceptional is the high quality and dedication of its community. Our members — spanning institutions, industry leaders, and economic experts — operate with a shared ethic: business development must be sustainable, ethical, and mutually advantageous. Through strategic matchmaking and close collaboration, we reduce barriers for French enterprises seeking foreign ventures and international partners looking to establish a foothold in Europe.

**Looking Forward with Confidence**

As we continue to expand our activities and strengthen institutional ties, I am deeply confident in EEAA's future trajectory. By aligning market opportunities with local community needs across Europe, Asia, and Africa, we are laying the foundation for a more interconnected, collaborative, and prosperous future. I look forward to working closely with our members, partners, and stakeholders to translate this shared vision into lasting economic success.
