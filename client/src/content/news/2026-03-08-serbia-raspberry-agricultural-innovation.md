If Serbia is internationally renowned for something with excellent quality, it is for its raspberries. As European–Asian cooperation extends into agriculture and food sectors, representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) met with the Institute for Applied Science in Agriculture, operating under Serbia's Ministry of Science, to explore investment opportunities and highlight local agritech potential.

Discussions focused on Serbia's raspberry production, a sector where the country holds competitive advantages in both quality and scale. The meeting emphasised how scientific research, sustainable practices, and technological innovation can enhance productivity, attract investment, and connect local producers with Asian markets seeking high-quality, niche agricultural products.

Delegates explored potential collaborations in agritech, knowledge transfer and joint projects aimed at modernising production, improving supply chains, and fostering innovation-led growth. The Institute's expertise was presented as a platform for bridging research and practical application, ensuring that investments translate into tangible results for both producers and international partners.

The meeting underlined that effective European-Asian engagement relies not only on trade and finance, but also on leveraging local expertise and sustainable innovation. Serbia's raspberry sector, combining tradition with modern scientific approaches, was positioned as a prime example of how regional strengths can meet global demand.
