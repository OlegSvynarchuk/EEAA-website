Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) held a meeting with Great Sports Media Co., Ltd., a leading sports media and event operations company in China and a wholly owned subsidiary of Shanghai Broadcasting Network and Shanghai Cultural and Broadcasting-Television Media Group (SMG).

During the meeting, the discussions focused on cooperation opportunities in sports media, content production, international event promotion, and brand development. Special attention was given to the role of major global sporting events as platforms for cross-border promotion, tourism visibility, and commercial partnerships.

Great Sports Media presented its extensive experience in sports broadcasting, signal production, advertising operations, and large-scale event management, including internationally recognised events such as Formula 1, the Shanghai Marathon, and the Olympic Games. EEAA representatives highlighted the potential for collaboration in promoting European sports events, cities, and brands to Asian audiences through media partnerships, content co-production, and integrated marketing campaigns.

The meeting confirmed mutual interest in developing strategic cooperation that connects sports, media, tourism, and branding, while leveraging major international sporting events as drivers of economic and cultural exchange.
