Representatives of the European Economic Alliance for Cooperation with Asia (EEAA) took part in a series of B2B meetings in Skopje, bringing together business leaders from Kazakhstan and North Macedonia. The meetings were organised at the invitation of the Ambassador of Kazakhstan to North Macedonia as part of a broader initiative to strengthen economic cooperation and promote new business partnerships between Central Asia and the Balkans.

The event provided an opportunity for direct discussions with Kazakhstani entrepreneurs and investors visiting North Macedonia, focusing on trade development, investment opportunities, and potential cooperation across a range of sectors, including industry, agriculture, logistics, and services. Participants exchanged insights on market opportunities and explored practical ways to enhance business connectivity between their respective regions.

During the meetings, EEAA representatives also received an official invitation from the President of the Astana Chamber of Commerce to visit Kazakhstan as part of a future EEAA business delegation. The invitation reflects growing interest in expanding institutional and commercial ties between Kazakhstan and EEAA member countries and opens the door for further cooperation, business exchanges, and investment initiatives in the years ahead.
